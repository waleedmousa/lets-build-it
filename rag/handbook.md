# Biscuit Labs Handbook
Biscuit Labs is a made-up company that makes Potsy, a smart plant pot that tells you when your plant is thirsty. Everything in this handbook is invented for a demo.

## Refunds
Customers can ask for a full refund within 47 days of delivery. No questions asked, although we are allowed to look a little sad.

## Returns
To return a Potsy, send back the pot and the charging cable. Please keep the plant. We do not accept plants back, because by then they have bonded with you.

## Big refunds
Any refund above 200 dollars needs approval from a team lead before it is paid out.

## Shipping
Shipping is free for orders above 60 dollars. Local orders arrive in 3 to 5 working days. International orders take about 12 working days.

## Lost packages
If a package has not arrived, ask the customer to wait 7 working days from the promised date. After that we reship the order for free, without asking for proof.

## Warranty
Every Potsy has a 2 year warranty. The ceramic Forever Pot has a 5 year warranty, which is a bold thing to promise about something called Forever.

## Battery
A Potsy battery lasts about 9 months on one charge. The pot blinks orange when the battery is low and blue when the plant is thirsty.

## Discounts
Only one promo code can be used per order. Students get 15 percent off with a valid student email.

## Support hours
Customer support is open Sunday to Thursday, from 9 in the morning to 6 in the evening, Cairo time. Outside those hours the chatbot answers and promises nothing.

## Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00. Outside core hours you choose when you work, as long as your team knows where you are.

## Remote work
You can work from home up to 3 days a week. Tuesday is the in-office day for everyone, because Tuesday is when the good biscuits arrive.

## Vacation
Everyone gets 26 days of paid vacation a year, plus their birthday off. You do not have to prove it is your birthday, but we will check the cake.

## Parental leave
New parents get 16 weeks of fully paid leave, whichever parent they are.

## Nap pods
There are two nap pods on the second floor. You can book one between 13:00 and 15:00, for a maximum of 25 minutes. Snoring is tolerated. Sleep-talking about work is not.

## Kernel, the office dog
Kernel is the office dog and attends the Monday standup. Any meeting that starts after 16:00 needs Kernel's approval. If he is asleep in the doorway, the meeting is cancelled.

## Meetings
Meetings last 45 minutes at most. Wednesday is a no-meeting day, so people can finish the things they promised in meetings.

## Deploys
Nobody deploys to production on Friday after 14:00. The last person who did still flinches when a phone rings.

## On-call
On-call is a weekly rotation that starts on Wednesday at 10:00. Each on-call week comes with a bonus of 150 dollars.

## Expenses
Lunch with a customer is covered up to 18 dollars per person. Send receipts within 10 days, or the finance team sends you a disappointed emoji.

## Learning budget
Every employee has a learning budget of 900 dollars a year for books, courses and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Your first month
You get your laptop on day one and a buddy for your first 30 days. Your buddy answers every question, including the ones you think are silly.

## Coffee
The coffee machine is named Gradient. It is descaled every Thursday. Whoever takes the last cup refills the beans. This is the only rule we enforce with real anger.

## Guest Wi-Fi
The guest Wi-Fi password is WaterYourPlants. It changes on the first Monday of every month, and the new one is written on the kitchen whiteboard.

## Security
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Lock your screen when you leave your desk. Forward suspicious emails to the security team instead of clicking the link to see what happens.
